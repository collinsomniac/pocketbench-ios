How to interpret

Comparisons isolate WebGL 2 versus WebGPU graphics-surface availability and callback pacing. A worker relay uses one outstanding message at a time and counts skipped pulses. WebGL and WebGPU use matching fragment formulas but different compilers; their draw rates are not a normalized GPU-performance ranking.

GPU timestamps are not measured on this page. WebGL timer queries and WebGPU timestamp-query availability are recorded independently. Draw submissions and animation callbacks cannot establish display presentation, input latency, or scanout timing.
